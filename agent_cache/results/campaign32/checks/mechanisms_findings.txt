RULED OUT:
  * (a) The read thread saturating and requests being admitted before their reads land: 0 of the 5,638 requests that computed history in the hinted arms (minutes 60-110) had a read parked or in flight at admission. The read thread was 75-92 % busy and starved by host-room refusals; its per-read speed drop (22 -> 13.6 K tokens/s) is its share of the saturated device (C1-C3).
  * (b) The SSD device slowing down as it fills, or cleaner effects: the ceiling stays 2,052-2,058 MB/s from first saturation to the end, with or without cleaner passes, and equals campaign 28's 2,040-2,056. Every flip precedes its arm's first cleaner pass by 7-46 minutes. Failed page reads are 0-1 per window and there is no discard traffic (C13).
  * (e) The engine choosing to compute on cost: no rule decision to compute exists (now 2 / wait 1,907 / arrival 3,152, no releases or bumps). Recompute comes from host-room refusals and cut reads (82-94 % of recomputed tokens) and from empty or capped lookups (1-16 %) (C7, C14).
  * Defect 1 (the three-lookup cap) as the main late cause: 'no hit' requests, mostly capped, carry 10-16 % of recomputed history in the gated arms and 0.7 % in oracle_v2; host room carries 82-94 %.
  * Cleaner deletions emptying lookups: ec_full's 'no hit' counts stay flat across its pass (29-43 per window), and 98 of its 111 no-hit requests in minutes 60-110 were capped.
OPEN:
  * About 0.4 M tokens of the host pool, beyond the GPU mirror (~645 K), read buffers and pins (135-182 K) and pending writes (0.24-0.48 M), cannot be separated from the logs: ancestors of pending-write or pinned nodes, anchors of parked reads, or evictable leaves. A periodic log of host locks by cause would settle candidate (c) exactly.
  * The no-rewrite and faster-device estimates (C16) are arithmetic, not runs. Testing them needs an arm that skips SSD writes for pages the store already holds (or marks recomputed nodes as stored), on the same users.
  * One run per arm: the flip minutes (68-86) and their order may move between runs, and no repeat of the saturated part exists.
  * GPU utilisation in the end state is not measured. Whether FA3 also co-binds at ~1 request a second while recomputing 12-15 K tokens/s is open.
  * The ~2.05 GB/s ceiling looks like a provisioned limit of the virtual disk vdc; it was not checked against the provider's specification.
