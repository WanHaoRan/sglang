   - **Hints don't change it:** the no-hint arm shows the same pattern (113 turns).
   - **Rule 2 likely won't either:** the no-hint arm has no early reads to release, yet 50 of its turns were refused.
2. **Per-turn overhead before the scheduler.** It grows with prompt length, from 0.04 s under 8K tokens to 0.17 s over 64K. I have not profiled it, but the server re-tokenizes the whole history each turn with one tokenizer worker.
3. **GPU memory in the episodes.** Fourteen minutes hold 89 % of the queue seconds, with the GPU pool 80–96 % full. Going from about 20 to 28 running requests adds about 11 % throughput while holding 40 % more of the pool.

**A better design**

- **One copy per page (my recommendation).** A page that is on the GPU and already saved to SSD needs no host copy; host memory then holds only sessions that are off the GPU, and a restore passes straight through to the GPU. In-memory capacity goes from 712K to 1,380K tokens, against a median live context of 1,176K. The forecasts would then decide which sessions get 712K of host memory instead of 44K. Your paper plan already lists the mirror as open item 4, blocked by the write-back lookup bug. The 100 GB arms (about 1–7 AM ET tomorrow) are the first test.
- **Use forecasts in aggregate (more novel, unproven).** Per-session gap forecasts bought at most 0.2 s, but their sum across sessions is much steadier, and the episodes hit every arm at the same minutes. Predicting occupancy 30–60 s ahead would let the engine act before the room fills, making rule 4 predictive rather than reactive. Agent-level admission that reacts to cache pressure already exists (CONCUR, MARS, ThunderAgent), so the novelty needs a proper check; I only ran a quick search and read snippets.
- **What I would not pursue:** finer fetch timing. Its ceiling here is about 0.2 s and the literature on it is crowded.

These breakdowns come from one run per arm on one workload, and the capacity gain is arithmetic, not a measurement. Details are in the last entry of `agent_cache/results/campaign26/DECISIONS.md`.
