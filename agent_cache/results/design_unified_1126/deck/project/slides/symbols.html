Token: one token of context, 98 KB of KV. Page: 64 tokens. TTFT: time to first token. Turn: one request of a session. Gap: the pause before a session’s next request.

A reference slide with every symbol used in the deck. On the left, the quantities of one session: the gap G, the time left until the return R and the idle time so far a; the arrival A, the queue wait W, and D or T, their sum, the moment the KV is next needed on the GPU; the hint’s gap distribution phi, with F, the chance of return within x, and h, the return rate; tau, the expected seconds a copy is held if kept x more, and tau star, the least holding time per return; the read time r, the wait in the SSD’s line V, the part of the read the queue does not hide x, and the landing time l; the stall S, the lost tokens U, the new tokens delta and the tokens of the prefix L; and the landing quantile q. On the right, the prices and system quantities: mu for a GPU-second; c for a second of stall; lambda and kappa for a host token-second and a second of SSD time; K zero and K one for restoring by a read and by recompute; the holding index nu; gamma and beta, the GPU’s seconds per token and the SSD’s tokens per second; and N, B, X, Z and g, the users, the turns running, the throughput, the mean pause and the GPU-seconds per request.
